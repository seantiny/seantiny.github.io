Overview

Designed, 3D printed, and tested a miniature wind turbine focused on balancing power generation, structural stiffness, and weight. We designed a three-blade rotor using a NACA 4418 airfoil with a 7.5° angle of attack and 15.3° twist, along with a lightweight triangular tower structure modeled in SolidWorks. We used FEA to evaluate the tower under a 9.81 N load, predicting a displacement of 0.459 mm and a factor of safety of 2.16. The final turbine weighed 251.2 g and was tested under approximately 24.5 mph wind, producing a maximum power output of 1.823 W. Structural testing resulted in an experimental stiffness of 14.22 N/mm, exceeding the required 8 N/mm target.
