Overview

One of my favorite classes I took at Berkeley and one I highly recommend. The course focused on understanding the finite element method from both the theory and practical side, covering domain discretization, interpolation, boundary conditions, global matrix assembly, and numerical solution methods.

Used MATLAB and FEMLAB to build and analyze finite element models across structural mechanics, heat transfer, fluid mechanics, and electromagnetics. Worked on a range of computer-based projects that involved constructing models, analyzing realistic engineering problems, troubleshooting simulations, and evaluating the accuracy of FEA results.
