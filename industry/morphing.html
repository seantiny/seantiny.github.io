Abstract

Achieving reliable morphing remains a fundamental challenge in 4D printing. Thermally actuated prints are highly sensitive to variations in printer hardware, material, and setup, causing identical designs to produce inconsistent outcomes. As a result, morphing performance depends less on design intent and more on setup-specific variations requiring manual calibration, posing a barrier for non-expert users. To address this challenge, we introduce ReTune, an end-to-end 4D printing tool that integrates calibration in the design workflow. ReTune leverages a multilayer gradient effect to establish a reproducible flat calibration baseline and combines it with inverse design methods to compensate for thermal shrinkage and curvature, enabling more consistent morphing across diverse printers and materials. We evaluate ReTune through a material study and a user study (n = 13), showing that users can calibrate their setups efficiently. By lowering the burden of achieving predictable actuation, ReTune supports the creation of accessible 4D printing for everyday makers.
