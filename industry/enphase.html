01

Materials Characterization

Due to NDA, images and videos will not be shared.

During my internship, I developed an automated testing workflow for ferrite core characterization to improve the efficiency and consistency of material evaluation. I wrote Python scripts to automate communication with laboratory equipment, enabling high- and low-frequency testing while reducing manual testing time and improving data collection reliability. To support the testing infrastructure, I designed custom printed circuit boards (PCBs) that interfaced devices under test with laboratory equipment, creating a streamlined and repeatable measurement process.

I also developed and maintained a centralized materials database with an integrated user interface and automated unit testing pipeline, allowing engineers to organize, validate, and analyze ferrite material properties such as permeability and permittivity for data-driven component selection and design decisions.

02

Power Electronics Hardware Design

Alongside my software and automation work, I contributed to the hardware development of an in-house modular power platform for next-generation microinverter applications. I performed PCB layout and routing for an ASIC microcontroller and housekeeping power supply, working within electrical and manufacturing design constraints to create reliable, manufacturable circuit boards. This experience strengthened my understanding of mixed-signal PCB design, power electronics, and the collaborative hardware development process from schematic implementation through board layout.
